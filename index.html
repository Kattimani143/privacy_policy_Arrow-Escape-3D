Privacy Policy — Arrow Escape 3D

Effective Date: October 23, 2026  |  Package ID: com.aistudio.arrowescape3d.vpxkqm

Privacy Summary: Arrow Escape 3D is a 100% offline-playable 3D puzzle game. We do not collect, store, transmit, rent, or sell any personal data, device identifiers, location information, or usage analytics to any external server or third party.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Arrow Escape 3D ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you install and play the Arrow Escape 3D mobile application on Android devices. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with all applicable Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Arrow Escape 3D does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect or access any of the following categories of personal or sensitive user data:

  • Personal identifiers (such as your real name, email address, phone number, or home address).
  • Precise or approximate geographic location data (GPS or network location).
  • Contacts, call logs, SMS messages, photos, videos, microphone audio, or camera feeds.
  • Advertising IDs, device fingerprints, IP addresses, or crash/usage telemetry.
  • Financial or payment information (all in-game items, skins, themes, and hints are unlocked solely using free in-game coins earned through gameplay).

3. Local On-Device Data Storage

To allow you to save your progress between sessions, Arrow Escape 3D stores game state strictly locally on your Android device using Android's standard internal application database (SQLite / Room):

  • Unlocked levels, star ratings, and best move counts.
  • Earned in-game coin balance, daily reward streaks, and achievement progress.
  • Equipped 3D cube skins and arrow color palettes.
  • Audio, vibration, language, and graphics quality preferences.

This data never leaves your device unless your Android operating system performs a standard user-authorized device backup.

4. Device Permissions

Arrow Escape 3D requests only the minimum system permission necessary for gameplay feedback:

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used exclusively to provide optional tactile haptic feedback when arrows escape or hit a blocked path. You can disable vibration at any time inside the in-app Settings screen.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Arrow Escape 3D does not integrate any third-party advertising networks, behavioral tracking SDKs, or external analytics libraries. No data is shared with third parties.

6. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Data

Because all game progress is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data at all times. You can permanently delete all saved game data by any of the following methods:

  • In-App Reset: Open the App, navigate to Settings → Reset Progress, and confirm the reset.
  • Android System Settings: Go to your device's Settings → Apps → Arrow Escape 3D → Storage and tap Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling Arrow Escape 3D from your device immediately removes all local database files.

7. Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape 3D is a family-friendly spatial puzzle game suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or process personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of the policy.
